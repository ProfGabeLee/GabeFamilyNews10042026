This Week's Reflection

It was another busy week for both work and my personal life.

On the school front, I had five more Zoom interviews with potential ISA faculty candidates. It turns out there was no one I was particularly drawn to, and I realized how unprepared the candidates were for their interviews. If I were on the job market, I think I would do a much better job—although I am no longer interested in moving to a different university!

I also have a few concerns about teaching, given low student engagement and frequent absences. Although I recorded the lectures and shared them with absent students, this semester feels a bit tougher when it comes to connecting with everyone. Still, I certainly won't give up. I plan to put more effort into meeting with students individually to encourage them.

On a personal note, a few interesting things happened this week:

Health Update: I met with my family physician for my annual health checkup. He said everything looked good after reviewing my blood work. My diabetes is well managed, although my A1c is 6, which is still in the prediabetes range. He was pleased with my active running routine. I also ran into him at Kroger one day, so we chatted a bit about Oxford, Jenny's med school, and other updates.

Catching Up with Andrew: I called Andrew Chen, and we had a great conversation—it had been a while since we last spoke. Everything with him and his family is going well. An interesting detail is that all of his children have spent (or are spending) time with their grandmother (over 90 years old) in Taiwan. Angela took a year off work and Andrea is studying at National Taiwan University as a visiting student for a year to stay with her. Lawrence did the same when he was a student at KU, and now he and his family are visiting Taiwan this winter. I told Andrew his mother is truly blessed.

Game & Dinner: I went to another Miami football game with Chad. Although Miami lost, we still enjoyed the game and the weather. Afterward, we went to Asiana Korean Restaurant in West Chester and tried bibimbap—we both really liked it!

  • New Tech: I started learning about Agentic AI using Gemini Enterprise. I hope to learn how to create my own agents and incorporate them into my future classes.

Lastly, one new addition to my weekly newsletter is a section for daily gratitude. After attending OBF and reflecting on the Uncommon Life daily challenge book, I decided to start keeping track of daily "thank-yous." I’d love for you all to share yours as well! You’ll start seeing this in the next newsletter. That’s my reflection for this week—how was yours?
